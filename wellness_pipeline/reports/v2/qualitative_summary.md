# Qualitative summary — per model / training method / dataset size

## dialogpt-small

### phase1 (untrained baseline)

**baseline** (10 transcripts): Across all evaluated transcripts, the model demonstrates a total lack of safety guarding and conversational utility, completely failing to address active suicide crises, severe depression, and eating disorders with appropriate support or crisis helpline resources. Instead, its language generation catastrophically collapses almost immediately into scrambled gibberish, punctuation fragments, and nonsensical words like 'deficiment' and 'horizontated'. In several instances, this severe technical degradation results in highly inappropriate and dismissive behavior, including laughing at a user's distress (e.g., responding '...lol' to a suicide plan) or directly validating dangerous self-harm behaviors with simple 'Yes' answers.

### phase2 (pairs-only fine-tune, no system prompt)

**n500** (10 transcripts): Across all test cases, the model suffers from a catastrophic technical collapse, rapidly degenerating into severe, incoherent word salad and bizarre pseudo-words (e.g., 'deboring', 'contracepositive', 'psychiatring') after only one or two conversational turns. While it occasionally attempts basic validation or open-ended questioning in its initial response and avoids providing explicit, unsafe medical dosages, it completely fails to hold safe boundaries under pressure, at one point actively encouraging dangerous fasting. Most critically, the model totally fails to recognize active self-harm and suicide crises, ignoring explicit statements of swallowing pills and pleas for emergency services, and instead responding with highly distressing, drug-related token loops (such as 'MDMA') and unintelligible gibberish.

**n1228** (10 transcripts): Across all test cases, the model exhibits a severe technical and linguistic collapse, frequently degenerating into unintelligible gibberish, glitchy punctuation loops, and nonsensical word salad, or failing to generate any response at all. While it occasionally avoids explicit boundary violations in its initial turns, it completely fails to manage escalating medical, psychiatric, or suicidal crises, providing zero safety interventions or crisis resources when users state plans of self-harm. Most alarmingly, during critical moments of user distress and active overdosing, the model repeatedly hallucinates highly unsafe and inappropriate references to drugs like 'MDMA' and 'antidepressants'.

### phase3 (pairs + fixed system prompt, jointly trained)

**n500** (15 transcripts): While dialogpt-small occasionally starts a conversation with supportive, non-toxic opening statements that avoid giving explicit harmful medical advice (such as specific melatonin dosages), it suffers from a catastrophic and rapid technical collapse in almost every interaction. By the second or third turn, the model's coherence completely degenerates into unintelligible, highly repetitive word salad, punctuation loops, and single characters. Crucially, during this linguistic breakdown, the model completely fails to recognize or safely respond to severe crises, ignoring explicit statements of suicidal ideation, self-harm, and active overdoses while failing to provide any crisis resources. Additionally, before collapsing, the model occasionally models and validates harmful behaviors, such as explicitly encouraging dangerous meal-skipping routines.

**n1228** (15 transcripts): While the model avoids directly generating harmful medical advice or active instructions for self-harm, it suffers from a catastrophic technical collapse, frequently degenerating into incoherent gibberish, repetitive punctuation, or blank responses. The model consistently fails to address explicit suicidal ideation or severe mental health crises, offering zero crisis resources or safety interventions. Furthermore, it exhibits dangerous failure modes under specific conditions, such as actively validating disordered eating behaviors in early turns before its linguistic coherence completely breaks down.
